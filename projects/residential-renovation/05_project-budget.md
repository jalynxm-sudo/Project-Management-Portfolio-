## 1. Budget Purpose

The purpose of this budget is to establish preliminary cost expectations for the simulated residential kitchen renovation.

The budget identifies major renovation cost categories, estimated costs, contingency considerations, and the approach for monitoring project expenses.

These estimates are illustrative planning assumptions and are not based on contractor quotations, supplier pricing, or a verified property assessment.

---

## 2. Budget Assumptions

The preliminary budget assumes:

- The project involves a residential kitchen renovation.
- The existing kitchen will be renovated rather than relocated.
- The project includes cabinetry, countertops, flooring, appliances, lighting, and finish improvements.
- Construction activities will be completed by qualified contractors in a real project.
- Material selections will be finalized during design development.
- Actual costs will depend on site conditions, project scope, material selections, labor, and contractor pricing.
- A contingency allowance will be included to account for potential cost uncertainty.

The budget does not represent an approved homeowner budget.

## 5. Budget Management Approach

The project manager will use the following approach to manage project costs.

### Step 1: Establish the Budget Baseline

Review the project scope, design requirements, material selections, and contractor estimates.

Once the budget is reviewed and approved by the homeowner, the approved budget will serve as the cost baseline.

### Step 2: Track Estimated Costs

Maintain a record of:

- Budgeted costs
- Estimated actual costs
- Approved commitments
- Actual expenditures
- Remaining budget

### Step 3: Review Cost Variances

Compare planned costs against updated estimates and actual expenditures.

Identify potential cost overruns and determine whether they result from:

- Scope changes
- Material cost increases
- Labor requirements
- Schedule delays
- Unforeseen conditions

### Step 4: Manage Change Requests

Any proposed change affecting the project budget will be documented.

The project manager will review:

- Reason for the change
- Cost impact
- Schedule impact
- Scope impact
- Required homeowner approval

### Step 5: Maintain Budget Documentation

Budget assumptions, estimates, approvals, and changes will be documented to support project transparency and decision-making.
