## Purpose

These meeting notes demonstrate how a project manager documents project discussions, decisions, open questions, and follow-up actions.

This is a simulated meeting record created for a portfolio exercise. The discussion points and proposed decisions below are examples, not evidence of an actual meeting.

## Summary

The simulated meeting focuses on aligning stakeholders around the proposed kitchen renovation scope, preliminary budget, schedule assumptions, material selections, and risk management.

Several items require further review before the project could proceed into an approved construction plan.

## Discussion Summary

### 1. Project Scope

The proposed renovation scope includes demolition, cabinetry, countertops, flooring, plumbing, electrical and lighting work, appliances, backsplash, painting, installation, and related project coordination.

**Discussion Outcome:** The proposed scope will be used as the working baseline for this planning exercise. Final requirements would need homeowner review and approval in a real project.

**Follow-Up:** Review the scope for missing requirements and confirm the final selections.

### 2. Design Selections

The planning documents identify cabinetry, countertops, flooring, backsplash, lighting, appliances, and finishes as key design considerations.

**Discussion Outcome:** Final selections remain outstanding in this simulated scenario.

**Follow-Up:** Document the preferred selections and evaluate cost, availability, and installation implications before procurement.

### 3. Preliminary Budget

The current illustrative budget is $42,350, including a 10% contingency allowance.

**Discussion Outcome:** The estimate remains preliminary and has not been validated through contractor quotes or supplier pricing.

**Follow-Up:** Obtain updated estimates and review the assumptions supporting the preliminary budget.

### 4. Schedule and Procurement

The preliminary schedule includes design, planning, procurement, construction, and closeout activities.

**Discussion Outcome:** The schedule requires validation against actual site conditions, contractor availability, material lead times, and applicable permit requirements.

**Follow-Up:** Identify schedule dependencies and refine the timeline when reliable information becomes available.

### 5. Risk Management

The risk register identifies potential issues involving hidden site conditions, material delays, cost increases, design changes, contractor availability, inspections, safety, and quality.

**Discussion Outcome:** The risk register will remain an active planning document.

**Follow-Up:** Review risks as new project information becomes available and document mitigation responsibilities.

### 6. Change Management

The change request log provides a process for evaluating proposed changes to materials, lighting, and other renovation requirements.

**Discussion Outcome:** Proposed changes should be evaluated for scope, cost, schedule, and procurement impacts before being incorporated into the project plan.

**Follow-Up:** Record the decision and update affected project documents after approval.

Follow-up meetings would be scheduled according to project needs and the urgency of outstanding decisions.
