# Residential Kitchen Renovation
## Homeowner Requirements & Project Scope

**Project Type:** Residential Kitchen Renovation  
**Project Status:** Planning  
**Project Role:** Project Manager (Simulated)  
**Project Approach:** Predictive / Waterfall

---

## 1. Homeowner Profile

**Homeowner:** Fictional Client  
**Property Type:** Residential Home  
**Project Location:** Fictional Property  
**Household:** Family household

This project uses a fictional homeowner to demonstrate requirements gathering and project scope development.

The homeowner is seeking a kitchen renovation that improves functionality, storage, organization, and visual appeal.

---

## 2. Current Kitchen Challenges

The existing kitchen is assumed to have the following challenges:

- Limited storage and cabinet organization.
- An inefficient kitchen workflow.
- Insufficient countertop workspace.
- Outdated finishes and fixtures.
- Limited visual connection between the kitchen and surrounding living areas.
- A need for improved lighting and functionality.

These conditions are preliminary project assumptions and would require a real site assessment before being used for an actual renovation.

---

## 3. Homeowner Needs

The homeowner would like the renovated kitchen to:

1. Provide improved storage and organization.
2. Increase usable countertop workspace.
3. Support efficient movement between kitchen work areas.
4. Incorporate updated cabinetry and finishes.
5. Improve lighting and overall visibility.
6. Create a cohesive and attractive design.
7. Include practical appliance placement.
8. Improve the overall experience of preparing meals and using the kitchen.

---

## 4. Design Preferences

The preliminary design direction will focus on:

- A warm, refined, contemporary aesthetic.
- Functional cabinetry and storage.
- Coordinated countertop and backsplash materials.
- Updated lighting.
- A practical kitchen layout.
- Durable and maintainable finishes.
- A visually cohesive relationship between materials and fixtures.

Final selections would require homeowner review and approval.

---

## 5. Functional Requirements

The proposed kitchen should include:

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| Cabinetry | Provide organized storage for kitchen supplies |
| Countertops | Provide adequate preparation space |
| Appliances | Support the homeowner's cooking and food storage needs |
| Lighting | Improve visibility throughout the kitchen |
| Workflow | Support practical movement between work areas |
| Flooring | Provide a durable and appropriate kitchen surface |
| Finishes | Establish a coordinated design appearance |

---

## 6. Project Scope

### Included

- Homeowner requirements gathering
- Existing kitchen assessment planning
- Kitchen layout development
- Cabinetry and storage planning
- Countertop and backsplash selection planning
- Appliance placement planning
- Lighting concept development
- Preliminary material selection
- Renovation schedule planning
- Budget category development
- Contractor coordination planning
- Architectural 3D visualization

### Excluded

- Actual construction
- Structural modifications
- Final engineering design
- Permit submission or approval
- Contractor agreements
- Actual material purchases
- Construction-ready drawings
- Final verified cost estimates

Any work requiring permits, engineering, licensed contractors, or other qualified professionals would be addressed separately in a real project.

---

## 7. Project Constraints

The project will be planned around the following preliminary constraints:

- Budget must be established before final design approval.
- Material selections must align with the project budget.
- Contractor availability may affect the schedule.
- Material lead times may affect procurement.
- Homeowner decisions must be documented.
- Construction requirements must be reviewed by qualified professionals.
- Design changes may affect scope, cost, and schedule.

---

## 8. Project Assumptions

The following assumptions will be used for initial planning:

- The existing kitchen will be renovated rather than relocated.
- The homeowner will participate in requirements gathering.
- Existing site conditions will be assessed before construction planning.
- Major material selections will be made before final procurement.
- Contractors will be identified during a future planning phase.
- The project will follow a predictive planning approach.
- The design concept will be developed before simulated construction planning.

These assumptions may be revised as additional project information becomes available.
